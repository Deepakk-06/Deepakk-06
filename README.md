<h2 align="center">⚔️ The Warrior</h2>

<img align="right" src="./assets/sentinel.jpg" width="260" alt="Sentinel" />

Final-year **EEE** student. I build robots for the battlefield nobody puts in the demo: **noisy sensors, drift, latency, and hardware that refuses to behave.**

I fight across **SLAM-based mobile robots, legged robots, and manipulator arms**, and right now I'm hunting one question: **does domain randomization improve generalization in VLA (Vision-Language-Action) models?** Weapon of choice: OpenVLA in Isaac Sim.

Simulation is the rehearsal. **Hardware is the war.** I'd rather ship something that survives a bench full of loose wires than something that only lives in a clean simulator.
